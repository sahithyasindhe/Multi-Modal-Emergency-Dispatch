## SOP-02 — Human-in-the-Loop (DEMO)

AI recommends; human decides. Approve / Amend / Reject / Escalate are mandatory before any conceptual dispatch action.

## SOP-03 — Duplicate Calls (DEMO)

Multiple callers for the same geo-temporal event should be clustered. Expand clusters to review individual narratives.

## SOP-04 — Low Confidence (DEMO)

If extraction or protocol retrieval confidence is low: abstain from assertive recommendations and escalate to human review.
