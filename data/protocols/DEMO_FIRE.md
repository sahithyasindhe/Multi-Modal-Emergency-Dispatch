# DEMO_PROTOCOL — NFPA-Style Fire Response Excerpts (SYNTHETIC)

> **DISCLAIMER:** Synthetic educational content only. Not official NFPA standards.

## FIRE-01 — Structure Fire Indicators (DEMO)

Visible flame, heavy smoke, reports of trapped occupants, and structural involvement increase severity. Kitchen vs structural conflicts must be shown to dispatchers.

## FIRE-02 — Resource Concepts (DEMO)

DEMO: engines and ladders for structure fires; rescue for trapped persons. Software must not auto-dispatch.

## FIRE-03 — Smoke Without Flame (DEMO)

Smoke alone may indicate hidden fire. Treat as elevated hazard until human clears.
