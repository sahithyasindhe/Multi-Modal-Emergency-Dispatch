## START-01 — Purpose (DEMO)

START-style categorization in this demo maps casualties into Immediate (RED), Delayed (YELLOW), Minor (GREEN), and Expectant (BLACK) for teaching multimodal triage decision-support.

## START-RED — Immediate (DEMO)

DEMO guidance: casualties with life-threatening conditions (e.g., major hemorrhage, compromised airway, significant burns, non-ambulatory with critical signs) are treated as Immediate. In SENTINEL-911, non-ambulatory / down casualties raise deterministic urgency toward RED.

## START-YELLOW — Delayed (DEMO)

DEMO guidance: serious but not immediately life-threatening injuries may be Delayed. Stable casualties who cannot walk but have controlled bleeding may map toward YELLOW in scoring discussions.

## START-GREEN — Minor (DEMO)

DEMO guidance: ambulatory, minor injuries. Highest-priority strike teams should not be assigned solely for GREEN incidents in this prototype's safety rules.

## START-BLACK — Expectant (DEMO)

DEMO guidance: expectant classification is sensitive and requires human supervisor review. This prototype never auto-dispatches based on BLACK labels.
